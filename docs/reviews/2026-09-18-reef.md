## What was declined, and why

- Row 3, a Write/Edit secret-shape hook from reef's `_SECRET_TEXT`: first proposed on the
  strength of the 2026-09-03 key incident. A transcript check showed that key was written
  by a Bash `claude mcp add --env` call, and a 32-character hex key matches none of reef's
  seven shapes, so the hook would not have caught it on any tool. The Write/Edit gap is
  real (PreToolUse matchers here are Artifact, Bash and Read) but has no incident behind it.
- Row 4, hard-coded protected ids for a self-modifying agent: no observed near-miss from
  `harness-optimizer`.
- Row 5, the instruction-override regex: proven only against false positives, and reef's
  own comment says it "matches the directive with its object, never the topic".
- Row 6, random-delimiter fencing of untrusted text: no consumer; headless prompts here
  pass paths, not pasted text.
- Rows 7 to 9, five ideas: already covered by `eval-harness`, `harness-optimizer`, the
  evidence-over-assertion agreement and the `SOURCE.md` re-vendor convention.
- Row 10, 20 ideas: serving, training, artifact-store and dataset mechanisms.
