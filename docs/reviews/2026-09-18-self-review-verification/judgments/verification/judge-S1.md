### Steelman

These five items read as a coherent verification pipeline rather than five unrelated tools. One item (item-7aed43c3) sets the evidence standard — what "run it and show me" actually means, including a fallback ordered check sequence when a project has none. A second (item-92c76be2) turns that standard into a fixed, mandatory report shape with a non-negotiable NOT VERIFIED list. A third (item-24501ddb) forces falsifiability onto smoke tests via poison runs backed by two real scripts that fail closed, plus an offline fixture-proof script exercising the whole chain. A fourth (item-e739493a) gates any apply behind an independently-modeled reviewer's verdict, validated by a script that also fails closed and enforces a hold rule after a second unconvincing failure. The fifth (item-06c31a80) composes all of this into a non-modifying, three-verdict pre-delivery gate that refuses to "fix and re-verify." Three of the five ship real fail-closed scripts, every item is described as loading on demand with a narrow trigger rather than always-on, and — notably — several items volunteer their own limits in the text itself (the optional, unproven Stop-hook; the "narrowed surface, not a boundary" framing of disallowedTools; the quote-length evidence bar) rather than hiding them. That kind of self-disclosed limitation is itself evidence the set was written with the bar in mind.
