Vessel series, ash and bronze

Atelier Ostra works between furniture and sculpture. Each piece begins as a drawing made on site, in the room it will live in, and is finished by hand over a season. We take four commissions a year. The archive below covers twelve years of work for private collectors, two museums and one cathedral.

Materials are sourced within two hundred kilometres of the workshop. Bronze is cast at a foundry that has poured for the same family since 1871. Timber is air dried for a minimum of three years before it is cut.
